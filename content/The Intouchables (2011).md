This was watched on the recommendation of my friend E, who said it was a light-hearted feel-good film for kids. While a little misguided, I would say most of that conclusion holds up. The trouble is that there are a lot of cheap jokes here, which seems to rely on us having a certain mindset to social issues that probably feel very 2011. 

Still, it's a heart-warming story with mostly very believable characters, portrayed excellently by Omar Sy and François Cluzet as the two main characters. Anne Le Ny also does an incredible job and seeing her character grow alongside Omar's brought much of the laughs this film gave me.

I found it somewhat thought-provoking, but it felt like they started out wanting this very touching film of this true story, and then had to add in some other stuff to please execs or to help create a flashy trailer (some crude jokes that honestly just make the Driss look completely unhinged, a sports scene with some sloppy camera-work which completely destroyed immersion and cheapened the surrounding scenes). I think other films do what this was trying to do better than this is able to. Would I recommend it? Maybe, if I didn't want to think about things too hard or perhaps if I saw this as a kid and was feeling nostalgic. 

Now, this will sound harsh, but I'm giving it a 4 out of 10. It could have been so much more.

Spoiler zone:

But honestly, if I could make one change to this film, I would cut the Magalie character entirely. It was just a vessel for poor jokes and it honestly would have been a better film with Yvonne in place of Magalie's story elements. 

This film does have some darker moments that made me think it's saying something deeper about this place we live in. After the film, friend E mentioned about how the two characters were both shunned by society in their own ways, and while that didn't seem the focus of the film, not I think it's main message (which I'm still thinking about), Driss and Philippe are both very clearly shaped by their places in society. Anyway, this darker moment was when Driss was stuck out all night hanging out with his friends. We saw Driss start to not enjoy where he was and what we was doing, saw his mood shift throughout the night as it became clear to us here was merely waiting it out until morning.
