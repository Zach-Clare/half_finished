*The Princess Bride's cousin, but a bit less shiny*

The sense of adventure here is fantastic, and things come together in a really excellent ending scene that really sticks with you.

The script isn't quite as squeaky clean, and there's quite a bit of shoddily concealed exposition. It can take you out of the moment at times, but the fun moments really outshine themselves.

My two favourite moments are, without spoilers:
- when two animals headbutt each other
- a puppet swordfight (very clever)

Overall, good fun but it defniately has it's imperfections.

Out of 10? Maybe a 7.
